BIOLOGYLESSON 01

Cell Structure and Organisation

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms. Let's get comfortable with the parts before we move to more complex ideas.

✳
What you'll learn

Identify the main cell structures, explain their functions, and distinguish plant cells from animal cells.

Start with the basics

Think of a cell as a tiny living workspace. Each structure has a job that helps the cell stay alive and function properly.

Cell membrane

Controls what enters and leaves the cell.

Cytoplasm

Where many chemical reactions take place.

Nucleus

Contains genetic material and helps control cell activities.

Mitochondrion

Releases usable energy through respiration.
